Sarvam 30B and 105B are Mixture-of-Experts reasoning models trained from scratch on large-scale, high-quality datasets. Both achieve state-of-the-art results on Indian language benchmarks and are competitive with frontier models on reasoning, coding, and agentic tasks. Open-sourced under Apache 2.0.
